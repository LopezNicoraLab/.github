## About Our Program

The Lopez-Nicora Lab advances soybean disease and nematode management through integrative field research, population biology, epidemiology, and reproducible data science.

Our work bridges discovery, Extension, and applied impact for growers across the United States and internationally. We focus on developing science-based solutions that improve soybean productivity, sustainability, and resilience.

---

## Research Areas

- Soybean cyst nematode (SCN)
- Root-knot, lesion, and reniform nematodes
- Sudden death syndrome (SDS)
- Emerging and re-emerging soybean diseases
- Population genetics and virulence dynamics
- Fungicide and nematicide efficacy
- Field epidemiology and disease forecasting
- Data-driven decision support tools
